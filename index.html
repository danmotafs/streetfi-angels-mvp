StreetFi Angels

StreetFi Angels is a SocialFi platform that transforms real-world informal merchants into investable digital identities.

What is it?

A platform that allows anyone to support local merchants through blockchain and digital profiles.

Who is it for?

  • Informal merchants
  • Tourists and locals
  • Impact-driven supporters

Tech Stack

  • Solana
  • Next.js
  • Node.js
  • AI tools
